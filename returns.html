Returns & Refunds

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Returns & exchanges

Contact us within 7 days of delivery if you would like to request a return or size exchange. Items must be unworn, unused, unwashed, and returned with their original tags and packaging. A request is not complete until TUCK confirms the return or exchange instructions.

Wrong or damaged items

If you receive an incorrect, defective, or damaged item, email support@tuckshop.in as soon as possible with your order reference and clear photographs. We will review the issue and arrange an appropriate replacement or refund where applicable.

Refunds

Once an approved return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the outcome. Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method. Your bank or payment provider may require additional time to reflect the credit.

Exceptions

Items that have been worn, washed, altered, damaged after delivery, or returned without the original condition may not be eligible. Any product specifically identified as final sale or non-returnable at the time of purchase is excluded except where the item arrives defective, damaged, or incorrect.
