Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Order processing

Orders are prepared after payment is successfully confirmed. We aim to dispatch orders as quickly as possible. During launches, holidays, or unusually high order volumes, processing may take longer.

Delivery

Delivery estimates and any applicable shipping charge will be shown during checkout or communicated with your order. Delivery times are estimates and can be affected by the courier, weather, public holidays, remote-area serviceability, or other circumstances outside TUCK's reasonable control.

Address & tracking

Please provide a complete and accurate delivery address, phone number, and pincode. Where tracking is available, we will share delivery updates using the contact details provided with the order.

Delivery problems

If your order appears delayed, arrives damaged, or is marked delivered but has not reached you, contact tuckshop.in@gmail.com with your order reference and relevant details so we can investigate.
