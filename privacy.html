Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Information we collect

When you browse or place an order, we may receive information such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order details, and technical information needed to operate and secure the website.

How we use information

We use information to process and deliver orders, provide customer support, prevent misuse, maintain the website, communicate service updates, and meet applicable legal or accounting obligations.

Payments & service providers

Payments are processed through third-party payment services, including Razorpay. TUCK does not need to store your full card details to process a Razorpay checkout. We may also share necessary order information with delivery, hosting, and other service providers that help us fulfil your purchase.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your orders, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern. Some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business, fraud-prevention, tax, accounting, or legal purposes.
