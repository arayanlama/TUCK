Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Before dispatch

If you need to cancel an order, email tuckshop.in@gmail.com as soon as possible with your order reference. We will try to cancel it before dispatch, but cancellation cannot be guaranteed once fulfilment has begun.

After dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched, it generally cannot be cancelled in transit. After delivery, any eligible request will be handled under our Returns & Refunds Policy.

Refund after cancellation

When a paid order is successfully cancelled and a refund is due, it will be returned to the original payment method. The time for the credit to appear can vary by bank or payment provider.

Cancellation by TUCK

In uncommon cases we may need to cancel an order because of stock availability, a material pricing or listing error, payment issues, or suspected misuse. If TUCK cancels an order for which payment was successfully received, the applicable amount will be refunded.
